# Deep research

## When to use
A question needing current, fact-checked, multi-source answers.
## Steps
- Decompose into angles; search each (deep_research / web_search).
- Fetch sources, extract claims, verify the doubtful ones.
- Synthesize a concise, cited answer; state uncertainty plainly.
## Guardrails
- Never invent facts or URLs; mark anything unverified.
