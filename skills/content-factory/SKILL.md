# Content factory

## When to use
Running a faceless content channel or marketing loop.
## Steps
- Pick a winning topic (from revenue radar / trends).
- Draft the piece (content_generate / social_draft), produce the asset.
- Schedule + publish (social_post), then measure and iterate.
## Guardrails
- social_post is consent-gated and verified pre-flight.
- One CTA per piece; consistency beats personality.
