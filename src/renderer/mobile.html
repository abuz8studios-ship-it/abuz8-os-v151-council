About

This is the ABUZ8 mobile app — a direct chat line to the sovereign agent running on your home server. The brain, vision, memory, and OS-control run on the server; your phone is the remote. Add it to your home screen to use it like a native app.

Tip: open it from your server’s address on Wi-Fi (or a tunnel when away). Voice input needs an HTTPS link; text + spoken replies work over Wi-Fi as-is.
